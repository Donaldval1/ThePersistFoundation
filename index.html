Who We Are

Persistence is a skill.
We help people build it.

The Persist Foundation was founded on a simple belief: success rarely belongs to the most gifted — it belongs to those who keep showing up. Our motto, “Until I Succeed!”, is a promise our members make to themselves, and a promise we make to them.

We connect individuals with mentors, resources and opportunities that turn determination into progress — in school, in careers, and in life.

Our Mission

To empower individuals to overcome obstacles and reach their full potential through mentorship, education and unwavering community support.

What We Do

Programs that build resilience

Mentorship

One-on-one pairing with mentors who have walked the road before and know what it takes to push through.

Education & Scholarships

Tutoring, workshops and scholarship support that keep learners moving forward when the path gets steep.

Career Readiness

Résumé coaching, interview practice and professional networks that open doors and keep them open.

Community Support

Peer circles, wellness resources and community events — because nobody should have to persist alone.
