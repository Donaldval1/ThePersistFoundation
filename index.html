Who We Are

Building the next generation of scholars and leaders.

The Persist Foundation is a scholarship and mentorship nonprofit based in Carmel, Indiana. We believe that when young people are equipped with financial support, guided relationships and opportunities to lead, they become agents of positive change in their communities.

Our motto, “Until I Succeed!”, is the promise at the heart of everything we do. Rooted in Indiana and connected to a broader national network, we are committed to raising up scholars, leaders and civically engaged citizens who carry these values forward.

Our Mission

To empower young people in Indiana and across the nation by expanding access to scholarships, structured mentorship, and civic leadership development.

What We Do

Programs that build resilience

Scholarships

We reduce financial barriers to higher education so students can pursue their academic and professional goals without the burden of overwhelming debt.

Structured Mentorship

We pair young people with committed adult role models who guide them in respect, financial literacy, emotional intelligence and purpose — through adolescence and into early adulthood.

Civic Leadership

From volunteer service to leadership training, we create pathways for young people to engage with their communities — so they become contributors to the public good.
